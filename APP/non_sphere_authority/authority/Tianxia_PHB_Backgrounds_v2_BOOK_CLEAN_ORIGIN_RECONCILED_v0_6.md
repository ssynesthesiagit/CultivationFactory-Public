# Backgrounds

A Background represents the mortal life that shaped a character before cultivation: family, labor, exile, study, hunger, craft, service, strange inheritance, social position, or survival.

At level 0, a character does not yet have a Path, Foundation, Method, or sect-trial Dao Sphere. A Background gives the character practical identity before those systems come online.

## Background Package

Each Background grants:

- ability score increases;
- skill proficiencies;
- tool, language, or trade proficiencies;
- a Background Sphere and Background Talent package, or a listed compensating benefit;
- starting equipment;
- suggested Origin Insight options;
- a social hook.

## Ability Scores

Each Background lists three ability scores.

Choose one:

- Increase one listed ability score by 2.
- Increase two listed ability scores by 1 each.

Species grants a separate ability score increase according to its own rules.

## Trade Proficiencies

A trade proficiency is a Background-granted proficiency representing a local craft, labor role, ritual practice, coded social language, or practical profession that is narrower than a full skill and broader than a single tool.

When a trade proficiency applies, add your Proficiency Bonus to the check. If a normal tool proficiency or skill already covers the task, use the most specific applicable proficiency; do not add your Proficiency Bonus twice.

## Campaign Languages and Cants

Backgrounds may grant campaign languages, cants, signs, or liturgical languages such as Classical, Trade Pidgin, gang cant, military signs, ancestral liturgical language, or regional ritual language.

These count as languages unless the Background says they are a trade proficiency.

## Background Spheres and Talents

Some Backgrounds grant a **Background Sphere** and one **Background Talent**. This represents mortal training, inherited practice, early exposure, practical discipline, strange literacy, family technique, weapon habit, craft method, or dormant spiritual sensitivity.

A Background Sphere is not the same as the Dao Sphere revealed by the sect trial. The trial shows how the character’s Dao begins to open. A Background Sphere shows what mortal life already taught them.

Every Background Sphere and Background Talent printed in this chapter is legal for that Background during normal character creation. Campaign-specific bans, additions, or custom swaps use the normal table rules, but a printed option does not require separate approval.

When a Background grants an **active Background Sphere**, the character gains that sphere’s base ability and the listed Background Talent. While the character is level 0, the sphere functions only at mortal scale. At CL 1, an active Background Sphere counts as one known sphere and follows the normal sphere rules.

## Background-Safe Talents

A Background-safe talent is a printed Mortal Realm or passive talent that can be learned at level 0 without granting CP use, Stamina use, Resonance use, full supernatural actions, companions, summons, area attacks, concentration effects, or high-Realm effects.

If a talent contains both safe and locked portions, the safe portions function at level 0 and the locked portions activate only when the character has the required Path, resource, Realm, or printed activation rule.

## Dormant and Preparatory Spheres

A **dormant Background Sphere** grants recognition, knowledge, signs, future access, and safe mortal-scale expression. It does not grant active sphere techniques until it becomes active through a printed rule.

A **preparatory Background Sphere** grants literacy, etiquette, offering practice, vessel knowledge, relationship preparation, or training habits. It does not grant a companion, servant, hosted entity, summon, or active sphere action until the character gains the appropriate Path, resource, and legal expression.

A dormant or preparatory Background Sphere becomes active when the character reaches CL 1 and has a legal Path, resource, feature, teacher, method, or other printed rule that can support that sphere. Once active, it counts as a known sphere and grants its normal base ability plus the listed talent, unless the Background says otherwise.

## Mortal-Scale Use

Mortal-scale use means passive knowledge, proficiency, recognition, ordinary tool use, mundane preparation, non-CP practice, or non-supernatural training.

Mortal-scale use does not include CP spending, Stamina spending, Resonance spending, sphere attacks, concentration effects, summons, companions, magical healing, area control, or supernatural travel unless a printed rule explicitly allows it.

## Variant Expressions

When a Background Sphere becomes active in cultivation, the player may choose a listed **Variant Expression** when the Background or sphere provides one. A Variant Expression explains how the same sphere appears through the character’s culture, body, Path, method, bloodline, tool, weapon, spirit, or personal Dao.

A Variant Expression does not bypass prerequisites, Realm gates, costs, sphere limits, Path restrictions, or printed restrictions. It changes expression and compatibility, not the basic legality of the sphere.

Custom Variant Expressions use the normal table rules for custom options. Listed Variant Expressions do not require additional approval.

## Origin Insight Options

Each Background suggests Origin Insight options. These are recommended matches, not exclusive lists. The Origin Insights chapter defines the mechanics for each Origin Insight.

## Abandoned Orphan

You grew up in alleys, ruins, kitchens, docks, roadside shrines, or abandoned courtyards. No one gave you a place. You survived by noticing danger early, hiding what mattered, and taking what you needed before someone stronger took it first.

### Ability Scores

Choose one:

- Increase one of the following ability scores by 2: Dexterity, Wisdom, Charisma.
- Increase two of the following ability scores by 1 each: Dexterity, Wisdom, Charisma.

### Skill Proficiencies

You gain proficiency in Deception and Sleight of Hand.

### Tool, Language, or Trade Proficiencies

You gain proficiency with thieves' tools and one language of your choice.

### Background Sphere and Talent

Choose one:

- **Scoundrel Sphere.** You gain the Scoundrel sphere and the **Grifter’s Hands** (`GRIFTER’S HANDS`) talent.
- **Scoundrel Sphere.** You gain the Scoundrel sphere and the **Hidden Tool Cache** (`HIDDEN TOOL CACHE`) talent.
- **Equipment Sphere.** You gain the Equipment sphere and the **Hidden Plates** talent.

### Starting Equipment

You begin with worn common clothes, a small knife, thieves' tools, a bedroll, a hidden pouch, three days of poor rations, and one keepsake from the life you lost or never had.

### Origin Insight Options

Suggested Origin Insights include:

- Street-Hardened
- Hunger Memory
- No One Saves You
- Quick Hands

### Social Hook

You know how people treat the powerless. Other outcasts may recognize you as one of their own, but officials, guards, and respectable families rarely assume the best of you.

At level 0, Spirit Companions does not grant a companion. Karma remains dormant until the character has a printed activation rule. These choices represent ancestral literacy, rites, taboos, and future access. When the sphere becomes fully active, choose an listed Variant Expression.

### Starting Equipment

You begin with plain ritual clothes, incense, calligrapher’s supplies, a family or shrine token, a small bell, a pouch of offerings, and a copied list of names.

### Origin Insight Options

Suggested Origin Insights include:

- Shrine Child
- Sincere Offering
- Family Ledger
- Grave Courtesy
### Social Hook

Families, elders, shrine attendants, and spirits may treat you as someone who understands obligation. That respect may also pull you into old debts.

## Traveling Performer

You lived by voice, movement, mask, instrument, joke, dance, story, acrobatics, puppets, or staged grief. You learned how crowds breathe, how nobles pretend not to listen, and how truth can survive when dressed as entertainment.

### Ability Scores

Choose one:

- Increase one of the following ability scores by 2: Charisma, Dexterity, Wisdom.
- Increase two of the following ability scores by 1 each: Charisma, Dexterity, Wisdom.

### Skill Proficiencies

You gain proficiency in Performance and Acrobatics.

### Tool, Language, or Trade Proficiencies

You gain proficiency with one musical instrument, disguise kit, or performer’s tools from the campaign’s tool, language, or trade proficiency lists.

### Background Sphere and Talent

Choose one:

- **Music Sphere.** You gain the Music sphere and the **Captivating Melody** talent.
- **Jester Sphere.** You gain the Jester sphere and the **False Opening** talent.
- **Puppetry Sphere.** You gain the Puppetry sphere and the **Little Servant’s Errand** talent.
- **Equipment Sphere.** You gain the Equipment sphere and the **Hidden Plates** talent.

If Music, Jester, or Puppetry later awakens as cultivation, choose an listed Variant Expression: performance, mask, satire, ritual theater, spirit-song, puppet-stage, or another culturally fitting form.

### Starting Equipment

You begin with performer’s clothes, a costume piece or mask, one instrument or performer’s kit, travel papers, a small blade, a collection bowl, and a rumor learned from an audience member who thought you were not listening.

### Origin Insight Options

Suggested Origin Insights include:

- Painted Face
- Courtly Caution
- Read the Buyer
- Vanishing Step
### Social Hook

Performers can enter places soldiers cannot, hear what officials deny, and vanish before blame settles. They are welcomed, dismissed, exploited, and suspected in equal measure.

## Glossary

**Scene.** A scene is a single continuous stretch of play focused on one conflict, challenge, investigation, negotiation, journey segment, or other meaningful situation. A scene usually lasts until the immediate situation is resolved, the location or objective meaningfully changes, the party takes a rest, or the GM declares that a new scene has begun. Some Tianxia features last “for the scene” or can be used “once per scene”; these effects persist through the current situation but do not automatically carry into later encounters, unrelated conversations, downtime, or a new combat.
